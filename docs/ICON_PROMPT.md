# Icona del gioco — revisione 0.2.3

Asset finale: `assets/icon.png`, generato con il tool **image_gen integrato**.
Riferimento di stile e identità: `assets/art/characters.png`, walker verde nella
sesta colonna. Il PNG generato è copiato nel progetto senza modificarne i pixel.

menu iniziale e nel piccolo marchio dell'HUD. Gli angoli mantengono la trasparenza.

## Prompt finale

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: final production game application icon, square 1024x1024. Primary request: a beautiful instantly readable icon for the cozy cartoon zombie management game named 'Niente Zombie Per Favore!' (No Zombies Please). Image 1 is STYLE AND CHARACTER REFERENCE ONLY: use the green zombie walker in the sixth column of its top row, not the other characters or atlas layout. Create a brand-new close-up app icon, not a sprite sheet. Subject: one expressive cute olive-green chibi zombie head with sparse scruffy dark hairs, big simple dark oval eyes and a slightly puzzled hopeful expression, peeking above a small chunky wooden checkpoint rail. In the foreground a large warm coral-red wooden STOP palm symbol / raised five-finger glove on a little cream circular badge politely blocks entry. The zombie's face and the stop hand are the two dominant readable shapes, arranged so the hand does not cover the eyes. Theme: a humorous gentle 'no zombies, please', friendly and playful not scary. Style: premium hand-painted toy-like mobile game icon, thick softly shaded dark-teal outlines, rounded sculpted forms, tactile painted wood and tiny brass fasteners, subtle brush texture, cozy polished illustration matching the supplied game's painted characters. Palette: sage/olive skin, deep desaturated teal background, warm cream trim, amber highlights, coral stop symbol. Composition: rounded-square deep teal tile with thick soft cream/ochre beveled frame, large centered zombie face filling upper half, stop hand and little gate filling lower third; clear silhouette at 32px, simple strong contrast, minimal detail, no distant scenery, no secondary characters, no text or letters anywhere (full title is outside the icon), no watermark, no gore. Lighting: warm soft amber rim light, inviting and charming. The full rounded-square icon is centered and fills canvas with only very small transparent corners. Actual transparent pixels outside the rounded tile.
```
